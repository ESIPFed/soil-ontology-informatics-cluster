# Publications

Papers, manuals, and reports that the
[ESIP Soil Ontology and Informatics Cluster](https://wiki.esipfed.org/Soil_Ontology_and_Informatics_Cluster)
cites when building the [Soil Data Landscape](../../Soil_data_landscape.md).

Each publication gets one card. Copy [template.md](template.md) and save it as
`<slug>.md`, where the slug is `<first-author>-<year>`.

`# Bibtex citation` is the only required section in the body and must hold a BibTeX entry in a
fenced `{bibtex}` block.
The BibTeX block and the `citation:` frontmatter describe the same work: write
`citation` for someone reading the card, and the BibTeX for someone importing into Zotero
or LaTeX.

For a publication without a DOI, use any stable URL for `resource`, such as a publisher or
agency landing page. Don't use bare DOIs without the "https://doi.org/" prefix.
