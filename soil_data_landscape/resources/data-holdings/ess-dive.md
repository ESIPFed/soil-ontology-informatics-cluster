# Description

The Environmental System Science Data Infrastructure for a Virtual Ecosystem (ESS-DIVE) is a data repository for Earth and environmental sciences funded by the US Department of Energy's Office of Science Biological and Environmental Research (BER) program. It archives data from DOE's Environmental Systems Science (ESS) research activities, with a particular emphasis on terrestrial ecosystem and soil data from long-term field campaigns. Major soil-relevant data collections include outputs from NGEE-Arctic (Next Generation Ecosystem Experiments — Arctic), NGEE-Tropics, and SPRUCE (Spruce and Peatland Responses Under Changing Environments). ESS-DIVE assigns DOIs to all datasets and implements community reporting formats to support data reuse and interoperability. It holds the CoreTrustSeal certification for trustworthy data repositories.
