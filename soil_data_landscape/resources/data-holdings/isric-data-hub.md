# Description

The ISRIC Data Hub is the central access point for global soil datasets maintained and curated by ISRIC — World Soil Information. It is built on GeoNetwork, a catalog application for geospatial data, and provides access to ISRIC's full portfolio of products including the World Soil Information Service (WoSIS) standardized soil profiles database, the SoilGrids global gridded soil property maps, the Soil and Terrain (SoTer) database, and an inventory of contributing organizations and their soil data holdings. The hub supports filtering by spatial extent, data type, and theme. Dataset documentation links to data dictionaries, methodology reports, and quality descriptions. ISRIC coordinates data contributions from national soil survey agencies worldwide, making the Hub a primary entry point for discovering what global soil data exists and who holds it.
