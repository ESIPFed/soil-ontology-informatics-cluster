# Description

The ORNL DAAC (Distributed Active Archive Center) at Oak Ridge National Laboratory archives and distributes NASA-funded data for biogeochemical dynamics and terrestrial ecology. Within ORNL DAAC, soil-specific holdings span field campaigns from boreal forests to tropical ecosystems and include measurements of soil carbon stocks, bulk density, texture, nutrient concentrations, and microbial communities. Datasets range from individual site surveys to gridded global products. ORNL DAAC also hosts the AirMOSS Soil Moisture Visualizer, which integrates satellite, airborne, and ground-based soil moisture measurements across North America. All datasets are assigned DOIs and accompanied by detailed documentation following NASA's metadata standards.
