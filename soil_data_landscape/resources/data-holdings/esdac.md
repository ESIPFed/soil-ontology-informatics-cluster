# Description

The European Soil Data Centre (ESDAC) is the thematic centre for soil data in Europe, hosted by the European Commission's Joint Research Centre (JRC). It provides access to a wide range of soil datasets compiled from EU monitoring programs, member state surveys, and scientific projects. Key datasets include the European Soil Database (ESDB), the Land Use and Coverage Area frame Survey (LUCAS) Soil data, the European Topsoil Survey, and maps of soil erosion risk, organic carbon stock, and sealing. ESDAC supports EU soil policy including the implementation of the Soil Strategy for 2030 and the INSPIRE Directive's soil theme. Data access ranges from open download to registered-user access depending on dataset sensitivity and licensing.
