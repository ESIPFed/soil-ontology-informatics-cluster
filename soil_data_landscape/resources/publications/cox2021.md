# Description

This paper from the CSIRO-led team articulates ten actionable rules for making vocabularies Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable (FAIR). The rules address everything from minting persistent, dereferenceable URIs for each term, to choosing appropriate serialization formats (SKOS, OWL), to establishing governance processes for versioning and community contribution. The paper is directly applicable to evaluating and designing the soil semantic resources catalogued in this bundle — including ontologies, vocabulary lists, and code lists. It is also a reference point for assessing the FAIR-ness of resources like AGROVOC, the USGS Thesaurus, and GloSIS codelists.
