# Description

These proceedings capture a National Academies workshop that brought together soil scientists, data engineers, and policy stakeholders to explore what a next-generation dynamic soil information system would look like for the United States and globally. Topics covered include the technical architecture needed to integrate disparate soil databases, the role of standards and semantics in enabling interoperability, governance models for sustained maintenance, and the use cases that should drive design priorities. The workshop is notable for bridging the scientific and policy communities, and its findings provide context for why efforts like the Soil Data Landscape resource cards are needed — specifically, the gap between existing soil data holdings and their actual discoverability and reusability.
