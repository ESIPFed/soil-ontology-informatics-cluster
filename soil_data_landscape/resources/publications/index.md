# Publications

Key literature on soil data informatics, data standards, and semantic resources.

# Literature

* [Todd-Brown et al. 2022](toddbrown2022.md) - "The promise of big diverse soil data" — synthesis on current soil data practices and the role of semantics
* [Onerhime 2021](onerhime2021.md) - "Data Standards for Soil: Why aren't they taking root?" — review of actors and standards in soil informatics
* [Cox et al. 2021](cox2021.md) - "Ten simple rules for making a vocabulary FAIR" — guidance on vocabulary design applicable to soil semantics
* [NASEM 2021](nasem2021.md) - "Exploring a Dynamic Soil Information System" — workshop proceedings on future soil data infrastructure
