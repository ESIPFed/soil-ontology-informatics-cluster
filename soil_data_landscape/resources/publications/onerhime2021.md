# Description

This paper reviews the landscape of soil data standards initiatives and asks why uptake remains low despite years of effort from international organizations. Onerhime maps key actors in soil informatics and data standards — including FAO, ISRIC, ISO, and national agencies — and examines both technical and social barriers to standardization. The review is particularly useful as a high-level map of who is doing what in soil data standards, making it directly relevant to the Soil Data Landscape resource card work. The paper identifies the fragmentation of efforts across jurisdictions and disciplines as a central challenge, and suggests that community governance and clear use-case motivation are as important as technical solutions.
