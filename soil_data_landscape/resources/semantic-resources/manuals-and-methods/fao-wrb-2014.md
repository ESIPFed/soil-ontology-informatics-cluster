# Description

The World Reference Base for Soil Resources (WRB) is an international soil classification system developed under the auspices of FAO and the International Union of Soil Sciences (IUSS). It serves as the global reference framework for naming soils and for creating soil legends for maps across national and regional boundaries. The 2014 edition (IUSS Working Group WRB, 2014) defines 32 Reference Soil Groups based on diagnostic horizons, properties, and materials. WRB is widely used in international soil science publications, global soil mapping projects (including GlobalSoilMap and GloSIS), and UN reporting. Unlike the US Soil Taxonomy (which uses a quantitative key-based system), WRB is designed for international applicability and comprehensiveness rather than strict quantitative thresholds.
