# Description

The Keys to Soil Taxonomy is the USDA-NRCS publication used by soil scientists to classify soils within the US Soil Taxonomy system. It provides a dichotomic key structure — from soil order down through suborder, great group, subgroup, family, and series — based on quantitative criteria applied to soil diagnostic horizons, features, and properties. The Keys are updated periodically to reflect new soil classifications and revised criteria. The current edition is maintained by the National Cooperative Soil Survey (NCSS) and is the operational reference for all official US soil survey classifications. The companion `SoilTaxonomy` R package (NCSS-Tech, GitHub) provides a computational implementation for applying the keys to databases. Soil Taxonomy classifications appear in SSURGO, NASIS, and the National Soil Survey Center datasets.
