# Description

The Environmental Ontology (ENVO) is an OBO Foundry ontology designed to represent environments and environmental processes. Soil is represented as an environmental material class (`ENVO:00001998`) with an extensive hierarchy of subtypes (e.g., forest soil, agricultural soil, permafrost) and relationships to processes (e.g., pedogenesis), habitats, biomes, and other material entities. ENVO is widely used in biodiversity informatics, microbiome research, and earth system science to annotate samples and observations with standardized environment terms. Its integration with other OBO ontologies (GO, PATO, RO) makes it particularly valuable for cross-domain data integration.
