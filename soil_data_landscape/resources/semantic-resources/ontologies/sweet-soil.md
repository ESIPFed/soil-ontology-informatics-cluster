# Description

SWEET (Semantic Web for Earth and Environmental Terminology) is a suite of ontologies originally developed at NASA JPL and now stewarded by ESIP (Earth Science Information Partners). The `realmSoil` module covers soil as a physical realm within the Earth system, defining concepts such as soil horizons, soil types, and soil properties in relation to other Earth realms (lithosphere, hydrosphere, biosphere). SWEET is designed for broad Earth and environmental science interoperability and is referenced by NASA and ESIP data systems. While less granular than domain-specific soil ontologies, it provides useful upper-level alignment for cross-discipline data annotation.
