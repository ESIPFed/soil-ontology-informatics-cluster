# Ontologies

Formal ontologies covering soil as a domain, expressed in OWL, SKOS, or related formalisms.
These provide machine-readable definitions of soil concepts and their relationships.

# Ontologies

* [ENVO — Environmental Ontology](envo.md) - OBO Foundry ontology for environments including soil as environmental material
* [SWEET Soil Realm](sweet-soil.md) - ESIP/NASA JPL ontology for Earth and environmental sciences, soil realm module
