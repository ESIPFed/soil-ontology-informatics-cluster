# Description

The USDA Agricultural Thesaurus and Glossary (NALT) is maintained by the USDA National Agricultural Library and provides a structured controlled vocabulary for agricultural sciences. It includes extensive coverage of soil science — soil types, soil properties, soil management practices, and soil biology — and is used to index the AGRICOLA literature database as well as USDA data systems. The thesaurus is structured as a hierarchy with broader/narrower term relationships, scope notes, and cross-references. It is published in SKOS format, making it suitable for linked data applications and semantic annotation of soil datasets. NALT is particularly important for US government data systems and for literature searches covering US agricultural research.
