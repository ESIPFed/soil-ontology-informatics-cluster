# Description

The USGS Thesaurus is a controlled vocabulary maintained by the United States Geological Survey to categorize USGS scientific information and help people and systems find collections of resources with common characteristics. It is structured hierarchically with top-level categories including Sciences, Methods, Topics, Product Types, Time Periods, and Institutional Structures. Soil-related terms appear under multiple categories — for example, soil chemistry, soil horizons, soil resources, and soil formation appear under Topics, while sampling methods and laboratory techniques appear under Methods. The thesaurus is widely used across USGS data systems (e.g., ScienceBase) and is strongly encouraged for metadata tagging of USGS-funded data. SKOS-compatible term identifiers exist in the underlying system, though the public interface does not yet expose persistent URIs for each term.
