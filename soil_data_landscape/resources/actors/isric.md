# Description

ISRIC — World Soil Information is an independent international soil reference and information centre based in Wageningen, the Netherlands. Founded in 1966 as a UNESCO centre, ISRIC has operated under Dutch government funding and hosts some of the most widely used global soil datasets and databases. ISRIC's mandate covers soil data collection, harmonization, and dissemination to support food security, ecosystem monitoring, and climate policy. It maintains the World Soil Information Service (WoSIS), the ISRIC Data Hub, and contributes foundational data to global initiatives including GlobalSoilMap and the FAO's Global Soil Partnership. ISRIC also plays a leading role in soil data standards development through contributions to GloSIS and OGC soil interoperability experiments.
