# Description

The Joint Research Centre (JRC) is the European Commission's in-house science and knowledge service. It conducts independent scientific research to support EU policy across a wide range of domains including agriculture, environment, and climate. For soil data specifically, the JRC manages the European Soil Data Centre (ESDAC), which acts as the central hub for European soil data, covering topics from erosion and contamination to organic carbon and biodiversity. The JRC also supports the implementation of the EU INSPIRE Directive as it relates to soil spatial data infrastructure, and contributes to the development of technical guidelines for soil data exchange across member states.
