# Actors

Organizations that steward, publish, or govern soil data and semantic resources.
Each actor card links to the data holdings or semantic resources that organization maintains.

# Organizations

* [ISRIC — World Soil Information](isric.md) - Global soil data custodian and research institute
* [European Commission Joint Research Centre](jrc.md) - EU scientific hub for soil data and policy
* [NASA Oak Ridge National Laboratory DAAC](ornl-daac.md) - NASA-funded environmental data archive
* [Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory](lbnl.md) - DOE lab hosting ESS-DIVE soil repository
