# Description

The Oak Ridge National Laboratory Distributed Active Archive Center (ORNL DAAC) is one of NASA's primary data repositories for biogeochemical dynamics and terrestrial ecology. Funded through NASA's Earth Observing System Data and Information System (EOSDIS), ORNL DAAC archives, documents, and distributes data from field campaigns, satellite observations, and modeling studies. Its soil holdings span hundreds of datasets covering soil carbon, nitrogen, moisture, texture, and microbiology across global ecosystems. ORNL DAAC also hosts the AirMOSS Soil Moisture Visualizer for North America and provides rich metadata and documentation standards that make datasets reusable across research contexts.
