# Description

Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) is a US Department of Energy national laboratory managed by the University of California. It conducts research in energy, environment, biology, and computing. For soil data, LBNL is most significant as the host of ESS-DIVE (Environmental System Science Data Infrastructure for a Virtual Ecosystem), the DOE's primary repository for environmental and earth system science data. ESS-DIVE archives data from major DOE-funded programs including SPRUCE, NGEE-Arctic, and NGEE-Tropics, many of which include intensive soil monitoring campaigns. LBNL also contributes to data standards and reporting formats for ESS-DIVE users, making it an active participant in soil data interoperability efforts.
